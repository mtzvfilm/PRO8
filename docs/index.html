New in 1.0.3: the Audio Unit version on macOS (Logic, GarageBand) no longer opens with a black panel; the seed preset banks are installed again for AU users.

Since 1.0.2: PRO8 for Windows. Windows 10 / 11 (x64): VST3 + CLAP in one zip, the same engine, panel and presets as on the Mac; projects and preset banks move between the two. macOS 11+ (Apple Silicon and Intel): CLAP + VST3 + AU in one installer. Also in 1.0.2: sustain pedal, arpeggiator and unison fixes.

A circuit-level emulation of the Behringer PRO-800, built side by side with a real unit: every stage of the signal path measured on the hardware and matched in the code. Source and details on GitHub.
